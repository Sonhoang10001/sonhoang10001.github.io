SECTION 01 • LOGISTICS & MOBILITY

Urban Transit Masterclass

Navigating Vietnamese streets alone demands zero friction. Avoid unofficial roadside touts and rely exclusively on verified app hailing.

Grab (All-in-One SuperApp)

Primary

The undisputed leader of Southeast Asian mobility. Features full English/Chinese interface and built-in real-time translation inside the driver chatbox.

• GrabBike (Motorbike): Fast and exhilarating; essential for slicing through traffic and plunging into deep alleyways (ngõ & hẻm). Clean helmet provided.
• GrabCar (Automobile): Recommended for airport transfers, tropical rainstorms, and carrying your luggage with fixed upfront rates.
• Payment: Seamlessly binds to international cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, UnionPay) to prevent cash-handling friction.

Xanh SM (Pure-Electric Fleet)

Clean Comfort

Vietnam's prestigious all-electric fleet by VinFast (cyan-teal livery). Zero exhaust fumes, completely silent, and air-conditioned to perfection.

• Zero Motion Sickness: Silent cabins with immaculate air quality—ideal if you are sensitive to traditional petrol taxi fumes.
• Standard of Service: Professional drivers in uniforms, disciplined routing, and transparent surge pricing during rush hours.
• App Option: Download the standalone "Xanh SM" app with full English language setup.

Airport 4G & Alley Routing

Connectivity

Instant mobile data is the cornerstone of solo urban exploration. High-speed 4G ensures real-time navigation inside labyrinthine alleys.

• eSIM Setup: Procure a Viettel or Vinaphone eSIM with a local phone number prior to arrival or at HAN/SGN airport arrival halls.
• Alley Navigation: Many spots sit inside ngõ or hẻm. If a car cannot enter, drivers drop you at the alley mouth; walk 20-50m inward.
• Sidewalk Alert: Keep phones held securely with both hands or tucked away when standing near curbs to prevent drive-by snatching.

SECTION 03 • GENTLEMAN'S DOSSIER

Field Notes, Cafes & Etiquette

Curated for an urban traveler attuned to architecture, subtle coffee spaces, and effortless interactions.

Architectural Coffee Sanctuaries

Just as Shanghai prides itself on minimal cafe enclaves along Anfu and Julu roads, Vietnam possesses a world-class third-wave and legacy cafe fabric.

Hanoi • French Villa Hideouts (Phan Chu Trinh) Seek secluded courtyards hidden behind peeling ochre stucco. Enjoy a single-origin cold brew or iced milk drip (Cà phê sữa đá) under rotating vintage ceiling fans.
Saigon • The 42 Nguyen Hue Cafe Apartments A 9-story mid-century residential modernist block transformed into stacked boutique indie cafes and vinyl listening lounges overlooking the pedestrian boulevard.

Currency, Cards & Etiquette

Rules of engagement across street counters and dining institutions:

Cashless vs. Cash Norms Boutique cafes and upscale restaurants happily accept international cards (Visa/Mastercard/Apple Pay). Street stalls and alley carts are strictly cash-only. Keep 300,000–500,000 VND in smaller banknotes (10k, 20k, 50k, 100k).
Wet Napkin Fee (Khăn Lạnh) At casual noodle stalls, sealed wet towelettes placed on the table often incur a small nominal charge (~2,000 to 5,000 VND) if opened.
Tipping Culture Tipping is not customary at street stalls. In speakeasy cocktail bars or private spas, leaving a 5-10% gratuity is welcomed as polite recognition.
